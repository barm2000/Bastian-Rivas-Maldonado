01 · About

Science meets computation.

My work is positioned at the intersection of environmental science and emerging computational technologies, integrating field-based understanding with remote sensing, modelling, artificial intelligence and three-dimensional data.

Research profile

I am interested in developing and applying computational methodologies to understand forests, watersheds, vegetation and environmental processes. My work combines traditional knowledge from forest science with geospatial information, remote sensing, hyperspectral data, machine learning and three-dimensional reconstruction techniques.

03 · Research

Current research direction.

My current interests explore how new representations of three-dimensional geometry can be combined with spectral information to improve the digital characterization of vegetation.

Current Focus

Digital Plant Phenotyping with 3DGS + Hyperspectral Data

Exploring the integration of 3D Gaussian Splatting, object-centric reconstruction, segmentation, hyperspectral information and computer vision for the development of new approaches to digital plant phenotyping.
